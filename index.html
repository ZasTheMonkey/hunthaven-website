FAQ

Common questions

How do I book private hunting land in Florida? +

Browse current listings, select an available date, review the property rules and full price, then continue to secure checkout. Availability and booking requirements vary by property.

Do I need a hunting license on private land? +

Florida state regulations apply to private land for most species. Regulations are displayed on every listing. Fully private, non-navigable ponds do not require a Florida fishing license.

What is the cancellation policy? +

Flexible — full refund if you cancel 48+ hours before check-in. No refund within 48 hours of check-in.

Can I list my land immediately? +

Yes — no approval needed. Create your landowner account, upload photos and details, and go live in minutes.

How much does it cost guests? +

Guests pay the listed daily rate + 20% service fee (on rate + cleaning fee) + Stripe payment processing fee (2.9% + $0.30). Optional trip protection available at checkout. No monthly subscription required.
